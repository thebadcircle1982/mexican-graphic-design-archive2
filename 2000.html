2000's

In the 2000s, graphic design in Mexico became more professionalized and globally connected. Designers worked across branding, publishing, and digital media. There was a stronger presence of design studios and a focus on identity systems, typography, and contemporary visual communication. Digital tools became standard, shaping both the process and the final outcomes of design work.
