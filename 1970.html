1970's

Graphic design in the 1970s became more experimental and political. Posters, independent publications, and printmaking were used to express social and political ideas. Designers such as Vicente Rojo and Rafael López Castro, along with various Chicano artists, contributed to this period through bold imagery and socially engaged work. Designers explored handmade techniques, strong visual language, and alternative forms of communication outside institutional systems.
