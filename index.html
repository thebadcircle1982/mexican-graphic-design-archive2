The Mexican Graphic Design Archive explores the visual culture of the country through its decades. Organized around individual design pieces, each entry documents its historical context and shows how design connects to politics, identity, and public life. The project aims to make the history of design visible and accessible. Click on the tiles below to explore graphic design by decades in Mexico.
